# Q4 Global Marketing Campaign Performance Report

## Overview
Our "Digital First 2026" campaign has been running for 3 months. While engagement is high, we are seeing mixed signals in ROI.

## Key Metrics
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** $145 (Target: $110)
- **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** 2.4x (Target: 4.0x)
- **Conversion Rate:** 1.8% on mobile, 4.2% on desktop.

## Competitor Activity
- **Competitor A (NexGen):** Launched a similar campaign with a focus on "sustainability."
- **Competitor B (Global Solutions):** Aggressively bidding on our core keywords.

## Issues
We are overspending on TikTok with very low high-intent lead conversion. Our messaging might be too generic for the current market sentiment.

## Recommendation Goal
Decide whether to pivot the remaining $2M budget to LinkedIn/Search or double down on TikTok with new creative.
