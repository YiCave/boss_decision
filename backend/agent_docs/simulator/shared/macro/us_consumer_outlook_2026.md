# US Consumer Outlook 2026 (Mock)

Synthetic macro context for simulator testing.

- Real wage growth: modest positive
- Credit card delinquency trend: slightly up in subprime cohorts
- Consumer confidence: mixed, improving in higher-income households
- Youth discretionary spending: volatile; promotional responsiveness remains high

## Implication for Pricing Decisions

- Broad-based price increases are more likely to hold in higher-income or high-loyalty segments.
- Budget-constrained cohorts require either:
  - value reinforcement messaging, or
  - targeted offers to offset churn risk.
