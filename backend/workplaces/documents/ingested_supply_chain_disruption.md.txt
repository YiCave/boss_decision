# Supply Chain Status Report: Port Congestion & Raw Materials

## Executive Summary
Ongoing strikes at the West Coast ports have delayed 40% of our incoming chipsets.

## Inventory Status
- **Current Stock:** 12 days of safety stock remaining.
- **Lead Time:** Increased from 4 weeks to 9 weeks.

## Financial Impact
Expedited shipping (Air Freight) will cost an additional $450,000 per shipment.

## Risks
If we do not secure an alternative supplier in the EMEA region by next week, production for the "Aero 7" model will halt.

## Question
Should we approve the high-cost air freight to maintain production schedules, or allow the production line to pause?
