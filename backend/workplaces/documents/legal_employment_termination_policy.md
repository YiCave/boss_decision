Document: Employment Termination and Due Process Policy
Department Context: Legal
Policy Category: Employment Compliance
Summary: Termination for performance must be supported by objective evidence, prior warnings, and a good-faith opportunity to improve. Recommended process includes written PIP with measurable criteria and review checkpoints.
Compliance Risk: Medium to High if due process steps are skipped.
Required Steps: documented coaching, warning records, PIP timeline, final review memo.
